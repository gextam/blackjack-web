What it is

An unofficial, play-money recreation of a Las Vegas Strip blackjack table in plain HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio and vanilla JavaScript: a six-deck shoe with a cut card, the dealer standing on all 17s, blackjack paying 3 to 2, double on any two cards and after splits, up to three hands, split aces receiving one card, insurance and late surrender. Three regulars play basic strategy with their own money so the table feels alive; a hint toggle shows you the book play and a Hi-Lo counter shows the running and true count.

Features

Real procedure

Cards slide from the shoe and flip with perspective; the dealer peeks under aces and tens, offers insurance, draws to 16, stands on 17; losing chips are swept, winners paid, cards go to the discard tray, and the shoe is cut and reshuffled.

Full rule set

Double any two, double after split, split to three hands, split aces one card, late surrender, insurance 2 to 1, table limits 5–500. Every rule is a switch in js/config.js.

Verified maths

node tools/sim.js plays twenty million rounds of basic strategy with the game's own rule code: house edge 0.40% (0.47% without surrender, 0.58% with the dealer hitting soft 17). node tools/test.js runs 49 unit tests.

A live table

Dana, Ken and Victor bet and play basic strategy with their own bankrolls; open a second seat to play two hands. The dealer speaks: bets, insurance, totals, busts, blackjacks.

Photoreal presentation

Generated court figures on crisp vector cards, a photoreal dealer in a live-stream panel, mahogany rail, printed felt, chip stacks and payout flights.

Full sound

Card slides, snaps and flips, the shoe, shuffles, chips, win chimes and bust thuds synthesized in Web Audio, plus the dealer's generated voice lines.

Run it yourself

Open index.html, or serve the folder (python -m http.server 8767 or start.cmd on Windows). The README has the configuration reference, the simulator and the headless test tools.
